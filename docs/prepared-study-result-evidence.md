# Versioned explanations for replayable prepared studies

The explicit source-evidence review now supports a complete local prepared study when its retained original is available under exactly one matching study ID. Before calculating, the tool captures bounded immutable source/prepared inputs, checks the recorded preparation fingerprint and replays the complete version-1 recipe. The prepared fields and every row must match replay. Missing, duplicate, changed, nested-preparation or explicitly nonlocal/conflicting sources refuse without rebinding or replacing the existing result.

The prospective analysis still runs from the frozen projection of fields/rows needed by the recorded query. Its integrity-sealed receipt additionally stores the exact recipe and original source title. The recipe includes original study ID/import event, original preparation fingerprint and ordered operations/raw codes. The analysis-input digest includes this lineage. Apply checks the complete live source/prepared bindings before and after receipt verification. Changing even an unrelated retained original/prepared value invalidates an unapplied preview. Source-reference normalization is checked and bound separately from the legacy preparation fingerprint, whose byte contract is unchanged.

Explain a result shows the recorded preparation source, import event, original input fingerprint and a complete read-only JSON recipe only after local receipt verification. A persistent summary receives owned focus if the recipe control disappears after a result/receipt change, without stealing deliberate outside focus or blur. The recorded facts describe the checked execution; they do not recheck today's library, authenticate a data source, prove representativeness or recover missing historical rows. A sealed recipe/fingerprint is not a backup of the original data. Existing original-study receipts remain compatible and have no preparation facts.

Supported calculations remain unweighted counts, percentages, means and sums, including recorded audiences/filters and overall numeric grouping. Remote, weighted, authored, calculated, wave and linked-intersection substitutions remain unsupported. This prepared-study path requires 1–50,000 rows, at most 200 fields and at most 5,000 output cells. Original/prepared full fingerprint inputs are each limited to the existing 20 MiB preparation budget; selected analysis inputs are limited to 20 MiB and recorded lineage to 64 KiB. The existing 4 MiB result-integrity budget still applies to the result and its lineage together. Capture, serialization, final binding checks and calculation still involve synchronous work. Cancellation propagates through preparation replay between its existing chunks; hashing/synchronous work is not interruptible. Native responsiveness/memory/Worker/CSP/background timing and accessibility remain unverified.

Receipts can contain source titles and exact raw recode history, stored unencrypted with the report; they are not anonymized. Up to 64 KiB per result may duplicate history and increase storage. Ordinary save status remains authoritative. No new source copies, migration, database, cloud sharing, credentials or permissions are created. Version-1 preparation retains its existing absent-property-to-empty-code rule; no new missing-property transformation semantics are introduced.

Verification covers four metrics, exact direct-engine parity, retained receipt persistence, invalid/missing/replaced sources, altered original/prepared values and metadata, wrong fingerprints/events, local-reference conflicts, oversized history, transient live mutations during hashing, cancellation, receipt corruption, case-varied untransformed keys, overall numeric summaries and the 50,000-row functional boundary. UI tests cover read-only complete recipe values, withholding pending/unverified claims and inside/outside/blur focus ownership. An actual App 1,000-row study verifies preview, Delete/Backspace guards, failed save/global Retry, Undo/Redo, same-process reopen, exact retained studies and immutable published history. No network calls, real respondent data, native quota/crash/multitab or Office/download acceptance is implied. Four initial model persistence assertions were corrected to match studies by stable ID rather than the storage normalizer's array order; full rows/fields/recipes/import identity assertions were retained.

This is prospective prepared-study lineage support, not historical reconstruction or full original goal 14 closure. Independent review is pending. The prior 91-file proposal, all frozen candidates, checkout, obsolete index and backups remain separate and unchanged. No publication, merge, deployment or blocked-build retry is included.
